How a round goes

  • 01
    Create or join

    One phone hosts. Everyone else joins nearby, or shares that phone.

  • 02
    Pick a pack

    Icebreakers, hot takes, story time, after dark — then a question.

  • 03
    Write in secret

    Answers stay hidden until the room is ready to reveal them once.

  • 04
    Guess the writer

    A correct guess pulls that player into your empire.

  • 05
    Conquer the room

    A wrong guess hands control over. Last empire standing wins.
