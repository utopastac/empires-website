How do we play?

One phone creates a game. Others join nearby over the local network, or use Add player so several people share a device. Pick a question, write secret answers, reveal them once, then guess who wrote what — drag empires together when someone is correct.

Friends can’t find my game

Make sure everyone is on the same Wi‑Fi or Personal Hotspot, and that Local Network access is allowed for Empires in iOS Settings. Bluetooth / peer-to-peer can help when Wi‑Fi isolation is on, but same-network Wi‑Fi is the most reliable.

Where is my data stored?

Game state lives on the phones in your session. Uninstalling the app removes local app data. Empires does not keep your answers on a remote server.

Something looks wrong

Tell us your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue happened. Screenshots help. Email archgrovehouse@gmail.com.
